## APPLICABLE REGULATIONS

| Regulation | Key Provisions |
|------------|----------------|
| **SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013** (as amended Nov 2025) | Defines "investment advice" as recommendation regarding securities with basis in individual circumstances |
| **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014** (as amended Oct 2026) | Defines "research report" and "research analyst"; requires registration for publishing research |
| **SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015** | Prohibits trading on material non-public information |
| **SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2021** | Applies to platforms facilitating securities transactions |

## FEATURE-BY-FEATURE SEBI ANALYSIS

### 1. Deep Research / V4 Intelligence

**Feature Description:**
Located in `backend/features/deep_research/service.py` and `backend/features/relationship_intelligence/service.py`.

**What it outputs (lines 145-295, 677-720):**
- Quarterly earnings trends analysis
- Valuation envelope (5-year P/E range with percentile)
- Fundamental relationships (Revenue→Operating→Net Income spreads)
- Financial health score (0-100 scale with pillar breakdown)
- Corporate actions/dividend timelines
- Peer group comparisons with median P/E and ROE
- Macro correlation matrix
- Anomaly detection (PE outliers, dividend yields)

**SEBI Risk Assessment: POTENTIALLY NON-COMPLIANT**

**Analysis:**
- The term "research report" under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 is **not limited to brokerage publications**. Regulation 2(1)(q) defines it broadly as "any written or electronic communication... containing an opinion or recommendation concerning securities."
- "Financial Health Score" is a **scoring system** that produces a numerical rating, which SEBI has previously regulated as a research output requiring RA registration.
- Peer benchmarking with "current_pe vs peer_median_pe" presents **relative valuation conclusions** that could guide investment decisions.
- The `report_generator.js` (line 212) states: "Notice: Generated by Mara. All metrics are derived from public filings and market data. This document does not constitute investment advice." This is **insufficient** as a disclaimer per Section 8 of the Investment Advisers Regulations.

**Specific clauses triggered:**
- Research Analyst Regulation, Section 2(1)(q): "research report"
- Research Analyst Regulation, Section 12: "Every research analyst shall obtain prior approval of the board"
- Investment Adviser Regulation, Section 2(v): "investment advice"

**Verdict:** `POTENTIALLY NON-COMPLIANT` — The structured comparative analysis, scoring methodologies, and valuation percentiles likely constitute regulated research services. SEBI registration as a Research Analyst would be required for commercial distribution.

**⚠️ UNVERIFIED:** Applicability of SEBI RA/IA regulations to a single-user non-commercial tool is **unverified** — legal counsel required. SEBI regulations assess **output nature** (scoring, benchmarking, valuation percentiles), not user count or revenue. A single-user tool producing "research reports" under Regulation 2(1)(q) may still trigger registration requirements.

### 2. Stock Screener

**Feature Location:** `backend/features/screener/service.py`, `backend/api/routes/research.py`

**What it outputs:**
- Multi-rule screening with AND/OR logic
- Pre-defined universes (US Mega-Caps, Indian Leaders, Semiconductors & AI, Crypto)
- Price, market cap, P/E, 52-week range for each symbol

**SEBI Risk Assessment: LOW-MEDIUM**

**Analysis:**
- Screeners that only **display facts** (price, P/E, market cap) are generally not considered "advice" under SEBI.
- However, the universe name "Semiconductors & AI" (lines 21-24 of screener/service.py) makes a **sector/technology claim** that could be interpreted as market commentary.
- If the screener were to produce **ranked lists or filtered recommendations**, it would cross into regulated territory.

**Verdict:** `LOW` — Pure data filtering without explicit recommendations or rankings. However, marketing language should be reviewed.

### 4. Backtester

**Feature Location:** `frontend/src/backtester.js`

**What it outputs:**
- EMA Golden Cross simulation (BUY/SELL signals marked on chart)
- RSI Mean Reversion simulation
- CAGR, Max Drawdown, Win Rate for the strategy
- Trade log with entry/exit prices

**SEBI Risk Assessment: LOW-MEDIUM**

**Analysis:**
- Backtesting is **historical simulation** of a trading strategy.
- The code clearly shows `BUY` and `SELL` markers (lines 72-73, 96-97) and calculates strategy performance.
- Section 2(x) of the Investment Advisers Regulations excludes "backtesting for the purpose of testing a trading strategy" from being "investment advice."
- However, if these results were **presented as likely future performance**, it would be problematic.

**Risk mitigation:**
- The tool is explicitly a "simulation engine" in the code comment (line 2)
- Results are labeled as "Strategy" metrics, not "Expected" or "Guaranteed" returns

**Verdict:** `VERIFIED COMPLIANT` provided the output is clearly labeled as historical simulation, not forward-looking performance prediction.

## RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SEBI COMPLIANCE

### Before Commercial Launch

1. **Disclaimers Must Be Prominent:**
   - Every "research" output must carry SEBI-mandated disclaimer per Investment Advisers Regulations, Regulation 8:
     > "Investment advice is offered subject to the client accepting the terms and conditions of the advisory relationship."

2. **Registration Consideration:**
   - If any feature produces **rankings, scores, or comparative analysis** that users could rely upon for investment decisions, SEBI Research Analyst registration is required.
   - See: Research Analyst Regulations, Regulation 9 — "No person shall act as a research analyst without obtaining registration."

3. **No Forward-Looking Performance Claims:**
   - Avoid language like "potential returns," "expected gains," "forecast," "predict," "guarantee."
   - Use: "Historical data," "Past performance is not indicative of future results."
