# MARA data and compliance

The portfolio deployment bundles synthetic data for research and education. MARA contains no order execution. Watchlists, research notes, and alerts in the portfolio demo reside in browser storage.

Provider-backed deployments have their own data permissions. Before hosting provider data, verify the rights for collection, caching, display, export, and redistribution. The NSE publishes its market-data policy. The yfinance project documents its intended research and educational use and directs users to Yahoo’s terms for Yahoo Finance data. [NSE data policy](https://betanseapi.nseindia.com/static/market-data/nse-data-policy) · [yfinance usage note](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance)

Review the intended research output with qualified counsel before offering it publicly. The relevant guidance depends on the service and its audience. [SEBI Research Analysts FAQ](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jul-2025/1753268710217.pdf)

A separately operated account-enabled deployment should document its authentication provider, hosting region, retention, and privacy contact. [Project compliance audit](compliance-audit/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md)
