2. Third-Party Processors

The demo does not connect to Supabase. If an operator enables accounts on a separate deployment, Supabase Auth handles account credentials and the operator is responsible for disclosing its selected region and processing terms. The static host may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Mara uses browser localStorage for watchlist, notes, alerts, and preferences. These are essential for functionality and are not used for cross-site tracking.

4. Your Rights

Use your browser’s site-data controls to erase the demo’s local data. For account or hosting records, contact the operator of the deployment you used.

5. Data Retention

Local storage remains on your device until it is cleared. An account-enabled deployment sets its own account-data retention policy.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and never transmitted to Mara's market-data API. All authentication traffic uses HTTPS.

7. Children's Data

The Service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.
