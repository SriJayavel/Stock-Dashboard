Based on a review of your current live deployment (**Mara Stock Dashboard**), here are the immediate technical issues, UI/UX bugs, and structural flaws present on your site:

---

### 1. Missing Data & Broken Rows (Data Pipeline Bug)

* **Empty Index Rows:** In the **Global Benchmark Indices** table, several major indices show empty dashes (`—`) for `LAST PRICE`, `CHANGE`, `% CHG`, and `UPDATED`:
* `^SSEC` (Shanghai Comp)
* `^GSPTSE` (TSX Composite)
* `^AXJO` (ASX 200)


* **Root Cause:** Your ticker ingestion or `yfinance` mapping logic is failing to resolve these specific tickers or handling `null` values gracefully.

---

### 2. Misaligned Ticker Categorization

* **US Equities in Indian IT Sector:** Under **Information Technology & Software Constituents**, `NVDA`, `AAPL`, and `MSFT` are listed alongside Indian equities (`TCS.NS`, `INFY.NS`, `HCLTECH.NS`, `WIPRO.NS`, `LTM.NS`) with the benchmark proxy set to `^CNXIT · XLK`.
* **Fix:** `^CNXIT` represents the Nifty IT index (Indian equities only). US tech giants should be categorized separately under a US sector benchmark (e.g., `XLK` or `^IXIC`) to avoid skewed sector analytics.

---

### 3. Telemetry Panel Overflow & Duplication

* **Duplicate Ticker Rendering:** The floating **Active Telemetry** panel duplicates the entire list of global indices twice in a single continuous stream (`^NSEI`, `^NSEBANK`, ..., `SOL-USD` repeated immediately back-to-back).
* **Viewport Occlusion:** The floating right-hand drawer (**Active Telemetry / Watchlist**) overlaps heavily with content panels on typical screen widths, making side-by-side reading difficult without explicit toggle controls.

---

### 4. UI/UX & Formatting Inconsistencies

* **Symbol vs. Format Mismatches:**
* Some cards render currency symbols explicitly (`₹23,856.65`, `$7,568.92`), whereas the ticker stream displays raw numbers or inconsistent sign conventions (`+ 0.19%` vs `+0.19%`).


* **Breadth Counter Mismatch:** The sector heatmap shows "Pharmaceuticals & Healthcare" with 6 constituents (1 gain, 4 loss, 1 flat), but labels it as *17% Breadth*. Meanwhile, "Banking & Financial Services" (3 gain, 3 loss) displays *50% Breadth*. Ensure your breadth calculation formula (`Advancing / Total Tracked`) is consistent across all cards.
* **Negative Value Formatting:** Negative change values inconsistently flip between using an en-dash/hyphen (`-`) and an explicit minus character (`−`). Standardize your numeric formatting function across all UI components.

---

### 5. Performance & Network Strategy

* **Polling Overhead:** The application uses continuous polling (`YFINANCE · LIVE · POLLING`). If client-side requests are firing directly to backend proxies without WebSocket streaming or response caching, hitting Vercel's serverless execution limits (10s limit on free/pro defaults) can lead to rate-limiting (`429`) or dynamic route timeouts (`504`).

---

### Recommended Action Plan

1. **Sanitize Data Ingestion:** Wrap your index fetching endpoint in a fallback block to hide or gracefully disable untracked assets (`^SSEC`, `^GSPTSE`, `^AXJO`).
2. **Filter Sector Components:** Split `^CNXIT` (Indian domestic IT) and `XLK` (US Tech) into distinct constituent arrays.
3. **De-duplicate Telemetry Arrays:** Fix the `.map()` or array concat logic in your ticker marquee / telemetry side panel.
4. **Standardize Numeric Formatting:** Implement a centralized `formatCurrency(val, currencyCode)` and `formatPercentage(val)` helper function.
